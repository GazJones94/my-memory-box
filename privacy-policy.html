My Memory Box Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

My Memory Box is a private memory-keeping app. This Privacy Policy explains how My Memory Box collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the app.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

This policy applies to the My Memory Box app. For privacy questions, support or a request to delete your account, contact us at MyMemoryBexDev@outlook.com.

Before publishing: replace the bracketed email address above with an email address you monitor. This address will be visible publicly on Google Play.

2. Information we collect

We collect the information needed to provide the app, including:

  • Account information: your email address and account identifier. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not visible to us.
  • Memory content: the photos, videos, voice notes, written notes, tickets, passes, PDFs and other files you choose to upload.
  • Memory details: titles, dates, stories, locations and the names of people that you choose to add to a memory.
  • Sharing information: the email addresses of people you invite to a shared memory box, and their access role.
  • Subscription information: your selected plan and, when paid subscriptions launch, purchase and entitlement status supplied by Google Play and RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details.

3. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • create and secure your account;
  • store, display, search and organise the memories you choose to save;
  • let you share a memory box with people you invite;
  • apply storage limits and manage subscriptions; and
  • respond to support and account-deletion requests.

We do not sell your personal information or use your memories for advertising.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only as needed to operate the app:

  • Supabase: provides authentication, database and private cloud-storage services for account and memory data.
  • People you invite: invited members can access the shared box according to the role you assign them. Do not invite someone unless you are happy for them to see the content in that box.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat: when subscriptions launch, these services process and verify in-app subscription purchases.
  • Legal or safety reasons: where required by law or necessary to protect users, the public or our rights.

5. Storage and security

Memory content is stored in private cloud storage and access is controlled by account and shared-box permissions. Data is transmitted using encrypted connections. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please keep your account password private and use care when sharing a box.

6. Retention, downloads and deletion

Your memories remain in your account until you delete them, delete the relevant box, or request account deletion. Where your account is over its available storage allowance, we may limit new uploads or make content read-only while giving you an opportunity to download your memories, as explained in the app.

You can delete individual memories in the app. To delete your account and associated memory data, email the contact address in section 1 from the email address linked to your account. We may need to verify your identity before processing the request. Some limited records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Children

My Memory Box is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will publish the revised version at this address and update the “Last updated” date above.
